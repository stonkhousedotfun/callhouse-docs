# Token supply and allocation

{% hint style="warning" %}
Stonkhouse v2 is unaudited. Interface v8 is deployed on Robinhood Chain 4663 from block 69,512,673 (22 September 2026); the launch markets are NVDA and SPCX. Registered markets can change; check the current app and on-chain status before trading. Operational services and market liquidity may be unavailable. Stock Tokens carry market and issuer risks. Buyers can lose their full cost; writers can lose collateral. Stonkhouse is not available to US persons. Read [Risks](risks.md) before using the product.
{% endhint %}

This page documents the current allocation of 25% of the STONKHOUSE token supply across the team, user incentives, and future growth.

STONKHOUSE is the ERC-20 at [`0xc2525b7c68b6d66dE5AABFEDC7B13314F389D5C4`](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/address/0xc2525b7c68b6d66dE5AABFEDC7B13314F389D5C4) on Robinhood Chain 4663. It has 18 decimals, and its total supply read 1,000,000,000 tokens on 22 September 2026. Half of the USDG the protocol's fee splitter distributes is set aside to buy STONKHOUSE and burn it, and the other half goes to the Treasury Safe; see [Where fees go](../product/where-fees-go.md). No distribution, buyback or burn has been observed on chain. There is no staking, and no contract pays protocol fees to token holders.

{% hint style="info" %}
The token and the protocol contracts are unaudited. The PinkLock links below are the lock records for locked allocations, and each lock's start and unlock date lives in its record; these docs have not read those records, so treat the terms below as the stated terms rather than an observed unlock date. Plans for additional team members, monthly vesting, and renewed locks describe intended future actions.
{% endhint %}
